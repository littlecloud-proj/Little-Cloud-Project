Little Cloud

A quiet prayer companion

Ecce nubecula parva quasi vestigium hominis. (3 Kings 18:44)

Little Cloud is a free prayer companion for any Catholic who wants a more contemplative life of prayer, modeled on the traditional rule of the Third Order of Carmel. It brings together the Little Office of the Blessed Virgin Mary, the traditional calendar, the day's collect, Epistle and Gospel, a rule of morning and evening prayer, mental prayer in the Carmelite way, and bells for the Angelus and the hours.

It follows the rubrics of Pope Pius XII (1955, with the restored Holy Week of 1956). The rubrics before 1955 are also offered.

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Everything is inside the app, which works fully offline. It is free and always will be.

Little Cloud is in testing on Android and iPhone.

Little Cloud is a private aid to prayer, not an approved liturgical book. Where you can, follow your approved missal, breviary and Ordo, and the direction of your priest.
