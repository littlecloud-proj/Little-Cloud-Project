Support

The quickest way to report a problem or ask a question is Settings → Send feedback in the app, which fills in your app version and phone model. You can also write to LittleCloudProject@proton.me.

The bells ring late on Android

On the Bells tab, if a card asks you to allow alarms, tap Open settings and turn on "Allow setting alarms and reminders". On Samsung phones also open Settings → Apps → Little Cloud → Battery, choose Unrestricted, and add Little Cloud to Never sleeping apps under Battery → Background usage limits.

The bells are silent on iPhone

On iPhone the bells arrive as notifications, so they follow the ring/silent switch and any Focus. Apple does not let ordinary apps ring through silent mode. Check that notifications are allowed for Little Cloud in the iPhone's Settings.

Which rubrics does the app follow?

By default, those of Pope Pius XII: the simplification of 1955 and the restored Holy Week of 1956. The rubrics before 1955 can be chosen in Settings. The calendar, collects, readings and Little Office all follow the choice.

Saying Matins and Lauds the evening before

On the Hours tab, set Matutinum and Laudes to "The evening before". After Compline they appear as the hours "of tomorrow" and count for the next day.

Where do the texts come from?

The Latin texts and calendar are from the Divinum Officium Project. The English readings are from the Challoner Douay-Rheims Bible, and the prayer book from public-domain manuals. No prayer or reading was written or translated for the app.

Little Cloud is a private aid to prayer, not an approved liturgical book. Where you can, follow your approved missal, breviary and Ordo, and the direction of your priest.
