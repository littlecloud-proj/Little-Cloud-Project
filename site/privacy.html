Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Little Cloud collects no personal information. This policy explains what that means in practice.

What the app collects

Nothing. Little Cloud has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. The Android app does not even have permission to use the internet, and the iPhone app makes no network requests.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, the hours you mark as said, your bell times and any prayers you write or edit are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are deleted when you remove the app. If your phone's own backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone), it may include this data as part of your device backup, under Google's or Apple's terms.

Permissions

The app asks to show notifications and, on Android, to set exact alarms and to start again after a restart. These are used only to ring the prayer bells you set. Nothing about your use of them leaves your phone.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback" in Settings, the app opens your own email app with a draft addressed to LittleCloudProject@proton.me. The draft includes the app version, your phone model and system version, and the rubrics you use, so problems can be fixed. You can read and change the draft before sending, and nothing is sent unless you press Send. Emails received are used only to answer you and improve the app, and are not shared.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: LittleCloudProject@proton.me
