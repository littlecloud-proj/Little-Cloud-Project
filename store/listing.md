**Full description:**

Little Cloud is a quiet prayer companion for any Catholic who wants a more contemplative life of prayer, modeled on the traditional rule of the Third Order of Carmel. Everything is inside the app: it works fully offline, has no ads or accounts, and collects nothing about you. It is free and always will be.

THE LITTLE OFFICE OF THE BLESSED VIRGIN MARY
• All the hours, Matins to Compline, in Latin, English or both side by side
• The right seasonal form for Advent, Christmastide and the rest of the year
• Bows, genuflections and other directions marked in red, as in the old books
• Mark each hour as said, set its time, and say Matins and Lauds the evening before

THE TRADITIONAL CALENDAR
• The rubrics of Pope Pius XII (1955, with the restored Holy Week of 1956), or those before 1955
• The feast of the day with its rank, colour and commemorations, from 2026 to 2050
• Fast and abstinence as the law stood, under the Code of 1917 or the American rules of 1951
• Our Lady of Guadalupe and an optional Carmelite proper

THE DAY'S MASS
• The collect, Epistle and Gospel in Latin and English (Douay-Rheims)
• Print or save the readings, or any hour of the Office, as a PDF

PRAYER AND BELLS
• A rule of morning and evening prayer you build, order and print
• A prayer book of traditional prayers, which you can search and edit, and your own prayers
• Mental prayer in the Carmelite way, with a timer that ends with a bell
• Real bells for the Angelus (the Regina Cæli in Eastertide), the hours and night prayers, with the wooden clapper in the Sacred Triduum

Every liturgical and devotional text is taken word for word from published, public-domain sources: the Divinum Officium Project, the Challoner Douay-Rheims Bible and old prayer manuals such as the Raccolta.

Little Cloud is a private aid to prayer, not an approved liturgical book. Where you can, follow your approved missal, breviary and Ordo, and the direction of your priest.

### Data safety
- Does the app collect or share user data? **No.**
- Is data encrypted in transit? Not applicable (no data is transmitted).
- Can users request deletion? Not applicable (nothing is collected; uninstalling removes all local data).

### App content
- Ads: **No ads**
- Target audience: 18 and over (general audience; not designed for children)
- Content rating questionnaire: Reference/Lifestyle, no violence, no user interaction, no data sharing → expected rating: Everyone
- Foreground service declaration (mediaPlayback): plays the bell sound the user scheduled when "Ring like an alarm" is on, until the user stops it.
- Exact alarm permission (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): rings prayer bells at times the user sets; the user grants it from the Bells tab.
